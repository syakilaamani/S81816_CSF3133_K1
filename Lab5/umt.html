Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university focuses on providing quality education in various fields, especially marine science, environmental studies and technology.

About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in 1979. It was established to provide training facilities for students and research facilities for lecturers in fisheries and marine science.

In June 1996, the institution became Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). It was later renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001. On 1 February 2007, it officially became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT).
